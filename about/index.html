Jovanna Venegas

About

Jovanna Venegas is Curator at SculptureCenter, New York, where she began her role in January 2024. Her exhibitions at SculptureCenter include "Pat Oleszko: Fool Disclosure" and "Akinsanya Kambon: Soul Sessions" (co-curated with Sohrab Mohebbi); "to ignite our skin" with new commissions by Elaine Cameron-Weir, Patricia Ayres, and Yu Ji; Luana Vitra: Amulets; "ASMA: Ideal Space for Music"; and "Open Process: Alexa West." Upcoming projects include "Berenice Olmedo: Epithumia."

From 2017 to 2023, Venegas worked at the San Francisco Museum of Modern Art, concluding her tenure as the Associate Curator of Contemporary Art. While at SFMOMA, she curated "New Work: Fernando Palma Rodríguez" (2023) and "Bay Area Walls: Liz Hernández (2021)" and co-organized the "2022 SECA Art Award"; and "Sitting on Chrome: Mario Ayala, rafa esparza, and Guadalupe Rosales" (2023-2024).  She also served as curatorial advisor for the Whitney Biennial 2022 on the U.S./Mexico borderegion andnd more recently curated the Platform section at the 2026 Armory Show, focused on the Caribbean. Venegas holds a BA in art history from the University of California at Los Angeles and an MA in curatorial practice from the School of Visual Arts, New York.

She will be the inaugural curator for the KTX Biennial in Dallas in 2027.
